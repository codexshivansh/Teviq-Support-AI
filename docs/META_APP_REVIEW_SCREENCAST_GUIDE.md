# Teviq Instagram Review — Easy Recording Steps

Meta ke form mein **Profile** aur **Messages** ke liye alag video boxes hain. Do chhoti videos banani hain. Neeche diye steps order mein follow karo.

## Record dabane se pehle

1. Recording ke liye alag browser profile kholo. Personal email, notifications aur unrelated tabs band rakho.
2. **Teviq dashboard ka sign-in page** kholo—yeh Instagram ya Meta login page nahi hai. Reviewer email se Teviq mein sign in karo; password record mein mat aane dena. Sign in ke baad **Teviq Meta Review** workspace khulega.
3. Do test Instagram accounts ready rakho:
   - Ek **Professional** account jo Teviq se connect hoga.
   - Doosra account jo test DM bhejega.
4. Pehle check karo ki Instagram connection aur AI reply kaam kar rahe hain. Nahi kar rahe hon toh recording se pehle issue fix karo.
5. Review workspace abhi Instagram se connected nahi hai. Production `@helloteviq` ko disconnect ya move mat karo. Alag test Professional account use karo. Agar `@helloteviq` hi use karna ho, toh pehle confirm karo ki doosre workspace mein connect karne se production par asar nahi padega.
6. Password, token, recovery code ya autofill kabhi record mat karo. Login screen par yeh maange toh recording rok do; login ke baad sensitive screen hat jaaye toh phir record karo.

## Video 1 — Profile

**Is video mein dikhana hai:** Instagram Professional account connect hota hai aur uska username Teviq mein nazar aata hai.

1. Teviq dashboard ke sign-in page par aao aur Record dabao. Yeh Teviq ka login hai, Instagram ka nahi.
2. Reviewer email se sign in karo. Password screen par na dikhne do.
3. **Teviq Meta Review** workspace dikhाओ.
4. **Settings** par click karo.
5. **Instagram Connect** kholo. Dikhाओ ki abhi koi account connected nahi hai.
6. **Connect Instagram** par click karo.
7. Test Professional account connect karo. Instagram password/login screen aaye toh recording rok kar sign in karo; safe screen par lautne ke baad phir record karo.
8. Teviq **Settings** mein wapas aao.
9. Dikhाओ ki Instagram connected hai aur account ka username visible hai.
10. Bolo: “Teviq yeh username dikhata hai taaki business jaan sake ki kaunsa Instagram account connect hai.”
11. Recording rok do.

## Video 2 — Messages

**Is video mein dikhana hai:** Test DM Teviq mein aata hai, Teviq reply karta hai, aur reply Instagram par pahunchta hai.

1. Connected test account ke saath Teviq kholkar Record dabao.
2. **Conversations** ya **Instagram inbox** kholo.
3. Doosre test Instagram account par jao.
4. Connected account ko ek simple test message bhejo, jaise: “What services does this business offer?”
5. Teviq par wapas aao.
6. Nayi conversation kholo. Sender ka username aur test message dikhाओ.
7. Teviq ke actual AI reply ka wait karo aur usi conversation mein reply dikhाओ.
8. Sender wale Instagram account par wapas jao.
9. Dikhाओ ki reply usi Instagram DM mein pahunch gaya.
10. Bolo: “Teviq Instagram messages ka use karta hai taaki businesses apne inbox se customer-support questions ka jawab de saken.”
11. Actual reply dikhne ke baad recording rok do.

## Recording ke baad

1. Dono exported videos shuru se end tak ek baar dekho.
2. Confirm karo ki password, token, notification ya doosre customers ki chats nahi dikh rahi. Zaroorat ho toh unrelated cheezein blur karo; test username, message aur reply readable rehne do.
3. Profile video ko Meta ke **Profile** permission wale video box mein upload karo.
4. Messages video ko **Messages** permission wale video box mein upload karo.
5. Abhi review submit mat karo. Provider countries aur tumhari final confirmations abhi alag se pending hain.

**Yaad rakho:** Render logs, Supabase, API keys ya real customer chats mat dikhana. Video banane ke liye production Instagram account disconnect ya move mat karna.
